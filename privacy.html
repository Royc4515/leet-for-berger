Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Leet-For-Berger is a free coding-practice site, run by one developer. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data.

Without an account

Everything stays in your browser: your progress, your submissions, your last session results and your code drafts are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. So are a few device preferences, such as your editor layout and which guides you have already seen. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.

When you sign in

You can sign in with Google or GitHub. Sign-in is handled by Supabase (our database host, in Frankfurt, Germany). We receive and store:

  • From your Google or GitHub account: your account id with that provider, your name and your email address.
  • Your practice progress, in one record that only you can read or write: which problems you attempted and solved, your submission counts, the days you practiced, the weekday you chose as your streak rest day, and your last 10 submissions per problem with their result and their code; and your quiz progress: which quiz questions you answered and how often you got them right, your best score in each quiz mode, and your daily quiz results with the answers you chose.

Code drafts you have not submitted are never uploaded; they stay in your browser. We use your data only to show your progress to you on your devices. We do not use it for anything else.

Reminders on your devices

Notifications are off until you turn them on, one device at a time, on your profile page. When they are on, we store with your account: that device's push address and its encryption keys (made by your browser's push service: Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, Mozilla for Firefox, Microsoft for Edge), the hours you chose, the kinds of reminder you turned off, your time zone, and a note of each reminder sent (the day, the hour and what it was about), so none is sent twice. The reminder itself is encrypted on our server so that only your device can read it; the push service only carries it. Turning notifications off on a device, or signing out on it, deletes that device's push address.

Running code on the server

JavaScript, TypeScript, Python and SQL run inside your browser. Java, C++, C, C# and Go are sent to our code server (behind Cloudflare, on an Oracle Cloud machine) to compile and run, and the result is sent back. The code is not stored. The server keeps a short technical log line per run: the time, the language, how long it took, whether it compiled and ran, and your IP address, to protect the free service from abuse. The log rotates automatically, so old lines are overwritten.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its editor and language runtimes from the jsDelivr CDN. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser fetches files from them, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Email LeetForBerger@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and your account and progress are deleted. Signing out alone does not delete them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: LeetForBerger@gmail.com.

Company names

Company names are trademarks of their owners. The site uses them only to say which companies asked a problem, from public datasets. It shows no company logos and has no affiliation with any company.
